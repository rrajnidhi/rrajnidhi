## About Me

I am a **UAV-focused robotics engineer** working on autonomous aerial systems that integrate **perception, planning, and control** into reliable, real-world deployments. My work emphasizes **robust autonomy pipelines** that operate under real-time constraints and scale from simulation to flight hardware.

I have hands-on experience with **PX4-based UAV stacks**, **ROS1 and ROS2**, and **multi-UAV system implementation**, along with deep learning–based perception modules for onboard sensing. I actively work on bridging the gap between simulation and real-world flight through careful system integration and testing.

My core areas of expertise include:
- **Autonomous UAV Systems** – PX4 flight stacks, ROS integration, and multi-UAV coordination  
- **Aerial Perception** – real-time object detection and tracking using deep learning (YOLO)  
- **Navigation & Path Planning** – classical planners and autonomy logic for aerial robots  
- **Sim-to-Real Transfer** – validating autonomy pipelines in simulation before deployment on hardware
- **UAV testing** - Testing the software in real challenging environment  

I enjoy working in **startup-style environments** where fast iteration, field testing, and system reliability matter. I contribute to **open-source robotics projects** and aim to build UAV systems that are not just intelligent, but **deployable, maintainable, and mission-ready**.
